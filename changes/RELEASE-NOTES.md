# Release notes

## 0.2.0

### Title

Ten rooms of heat

### Body

- Dash through ice and use the opening until your next retry.
- Catch a hollow diamond to dash again.
- Unlock diagonal dashes and leave temporary ramps.
- Bait seekers with a hot streak, then move before they charge.
- Take the resting ledges or try a faster aerial route.
- Finish with one room that brings the mechanics together.
