# Episodes

The game now has ten rooms. The expansion was built before further promotion,
not filmed retroactively. Episode 1 still refers to the original four-room
build: `ep01-start` and `ep01-end` are immutable.

Development milestones for the expansion are separate from episode tags.
An episode gets start/end tags only when its actual lesson boundaries are set.
The [original backlog](EPISODES-ORIGINAL.md) is archived for reference, not queued
work. No cold pulses, boss, refreezing ice, or mid-room checkpoints are in the
current scope.

## 1. Put the SDK in and count the deaths

Keep the original [prompts](../prompts/01-put-the-sdk-in.md) and tags intact.
The four-room build reports deaths by cause, room, and position. Its deliberately
hard third room belongs to that build. Do not present it as the expanded game's
third room or mix death counts from different versions.

one question to investigate from player behavior. Prepare its prompts and exact
tag boundaries before filming.
